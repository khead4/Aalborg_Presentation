Research agenda

What I want to study is not whether AI can produce more output.

I want to study whether HCT-assisted interaction can help architects sustain continuity, interpret evidence, revise with confidence, and protect professional agency.

RQ focusHow fragmentation affects continuity
Prototype focusHow support changes reasoning and revision
Evaluation focusQuality, trust, cognitive load, decision traceability
Final line: I built the prototype to make the research question observable. The goal is not to automate architecture, but to study how AI can support better human judgment inside architectural practice.
